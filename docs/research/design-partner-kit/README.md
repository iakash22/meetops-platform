# Design-partner kit (India SMB and mid-market)

Material for running phase 0 of the India launch plan: 5–10 design-partner teams, free and hands-on, with Hindi-English
eval data collected under consent ([gtm-india-smb-midmarket.md](../gtm-india-smb-midmarket.md) §8). These docs are not
specs and decide nothing. **Proposal** marks our suggestions, **Interpretation** marks our reading of a source, and
**Decision needed** marks a choice for Akash or the AI Engineer.

- **Checked:** 2026-11-12 (IST). Repo paths are at `integration/phase-1` `d50d0dc2`.
- **No outreach.** The kit names no real people and lists no real companies as targets. Organisations are described by
  segment or archetype only. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

| Doc                                                              | What it covers                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [icp-and-recruitment.md](icp-and-recruitment.md)                 | ICP by segment and role, qualification, disqualifiers, cohort size and mix, incentives, generic channels |
| [interview-script.md](interview-script.md)                       | Discovery and pilot interview scripts with consent-to-record wording and probes tied to gaps and P0 bets |
| [pilot-success-metrics.md](pilot-success-metrics.md)             | Metric definitions, formulas, baseline capture, proposed targets and the product tables they come from   |
| [pilot-agreement-outline.md](pilot-agreement-outline.md)         | Pilot agreement headings mapped to the India DPA outline. Outline, not legal advice                      |
| [hinglish-eval-data-protocol.md](hinglish-eval-data-protocol.md) | Consented Hindi-English eval data: consent text, de-identification, retention, access, storage, fixtures |

Decisions the kit needs are collected in [hinglish-eval-data-protocol.md](hinglish-eval-data-protocol.md) §10 and at the
end of each doc.
