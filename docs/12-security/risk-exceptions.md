# Risk exceptions register

This register lists known security risks that we accept for a limited time instead of fixing them right away.

## Process

- Every exception needs a named owner, an acceptance date, the person who accepted it, and a review date.
- The owner reviews it again on or before the review date. At that review it is either closed (fixed), renewed with a new review date and a written reason, or escalated. An exception past its review date counts as expired, and the finding blocks PRs again.
- Each entry states its scope (where the risk applies and where it must not), the justification, how that was verified, and an exit criterion.
- A high or critical `pnpm audit` alert blocks a PR unless it has an active entry here **and** a matching row in the `EXCEPTIONS` list of `scripts/audit-gate.mjs` (same ID, GHSA, module, exact versions and expiry). The gate stops excepting the finding after the expiry timestamp, so CI fails again.
- Never put secrets, tokens or credentials in this file.
